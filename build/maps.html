Rules (please read before submitting a map):

  • You can only upload max. 3 maps per day.
  • Maps must at least have 50 unique locations.
  • Ensure that your map names and author names are familiy-friendly.
  • Pick a descriptive map name (e. g. not "My Map")
  • Community Maps are not meant for country maps or other maps that can easily be generated. They are meant for special places.
  • A maximum of 1000 locations is recommended.
  • Maps cannot be edited or deleted! Message us on the Discord server if you need to delete one.
